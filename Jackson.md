# Zippy Villains — Design Direction

## Scope
- Zippy villains will be original characters inspired by villain archetypes, not named copies of copyrighted characters.
- Strength: lean, dramatic, and energetic; villains may read as imposing or sinister rather than muscular.
- Villains are distinct from heroes through silhouette, posture, face, color, and motion.

## Planned Character System
- 10 villains in the first villain roster.
- Shared SVG/CSS character engine with the hero roster.
- Customizable palette, expressions, accessories, and animation states.
- Animated idle, speak, taunt/cheer, and alert reactions.
- Settings preview and immediate live update.

## First Villain Pass
1. Create each villain's original name, archetype, silhouette, palette, and personality.
2. Add the villain roster to Zippy's character selection.
3. Preserve the existing hero roster and add category navigation.
4. Ensure villains remain original and avoid direct visual imitation of existing copyrighted characters.
